# Authoring guidance — briefing

Author Markdown only. Never hand-author HTML or generated presentation markup.

Write an executive briefing that a busy reader can scan, understand, and act on.

Use this structure:

## Headline
One sentence stating the current situation and why it matters now.

## At a glance
Three to five bullets covering outcome, progress, timing, and the most important signal.

## Decisions or support needed
Name each ask, its owner, and the date by which it is needed. Say "None" when there is
no ask.

## Current picture
Summarize evidence and progress. Use a compact table when comparing milestones,
metrics, workstreams, or owners.

## Risks and next moves
Pair each material risk with a response, then list the next two or three actions.

Rules:

- Lead with implications, not chronology.
- Distinguish observed facts from forecasts.
- Define acronyms and avoid implementation detail unless it changes a decision.
- Keep presentation color-free so the rendered document works in light and dark themes.
