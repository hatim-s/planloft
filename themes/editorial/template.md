# Authoring guidance — editorial

Write the plan like a short, confident engineering article a reader enjoys.

Shape:

- **TL;DR** — 2–3 sentences at the top: what we're doing and why it's the right call.
- A narrative that flows: the situation → the tension/problem → the approach → what
  changes. Use prose paragraphs, not just bullets.
- Use `>` callouts for the one or two ideas that matter most.
- Fold the concrete steps into a clearly-marked section near the end so the reader can
  act after the story.

Voice:

- Active, direct, a little opinionated. Explain the *why*, not just the *what*.
- Short paragraphs. Vary sentence length.
- One strong closing line on the expected outcome.
