# Authoring guidance — decision

Author Markdown only. Never hand-author HTML or generated presentation markup.

Write a durable decision record that explains what was decided and makes the reasoning
auditable later.

Use this structure:

## Decision
State the choice in one direct paragraph. Include status and decision date when known.

## Context and constraints
Describe the forces that shaped the choice, including non-negotiable technical,
operational, security, cost, or timing constraints.

## Options considered
Compare the credible options against explicit criteria. Use a table when it makes the
trade-offs easier to verify.

## Rationale
Explain why the selected option best fits the criteria. Separate evidence from
judgment and record meaningful dissent or uncertainty.

## Consequences
List benefits, costs, risks, follow-up work, and the conditions that would justify
revisiting the decision.

Rules:

- Record the decision, not a meeting transcript.
- Include rejected options and why they lost.
- Use falsifiable claims and link supporting evidence when available.
- Keep presentation color-free so the rendered document works in light and dark themes.
